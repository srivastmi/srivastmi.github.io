How It Went

🏊 Swim 38:42 · 2:00/100m · 2nd age group

Elite for the field — exited the water 5th out of everyone. Controlled, strong, and a massive early buffer. This is the standout leg and the biggest asset to build on.

🚴 Bike 3:27:07 · 16.2 mph · 3,724 ft climbing

A genuinely hilly course — nearly 3,700 ft of climbing explains the speed. Ridden a touch hot: avg HR 149 vs the ~135 Z2 race-effort target. That extra 10–15 bpm over three and a half hours is what pre-fatigued the legs for the run.

🏃 Run 2:14:10 · 10:14/mi · 948 ft

Cardio was never the problem — HR sat at a sustainable 151 and breathing was fine the whole way. The limiter was purely muscular: quads seizing and calves cramping from the hot, hilly bike plus under-dosed sodium (~200 mg/hr). Ran every step of it home anyway — no walking it in.

The unlock for next time

An engine that can run a 3:53 marathon and a swim that places top-5 in the field — the race was left on the bike, not the body. Ride 10–15 bpm easier, take in 500–700 mg sodium/hr, and that run turns from 10:14 survival into ~9:00–9:30 cruising — worth roughly 15–20 minutes faster overall. A controllable fix, not a fitness gap.

Next up: regain upper-body strength, chase the dunk, sharpen short-distance speed — then build toward a full Ironman. See the training system.
