Strength Routine

Maintain upper/lower mass, build resilient marathon legs, support speed. Run-priority scheduling: gym never the day before the long run or a hard session.

Fueling (modern carb strategy)

  • During long runs & race: 60–90g carbs/hour. A GU is ~22g — that's 3 gels/hour, not 1 every 45 min.
  • Train the gut: ramp from 1 gel/30min now to 3/hour by Week 12. If stomach revolts, scale back and retry.
  • Race day: gel every ~20–25 min with water. Glucose-fructose blend gels (Maurten/SiS) absorb better than pure glucose.
  • Carb load: Thu–Sun before race, ~3.5g carbs per lb bodyweight on the final 2 days.
  • Daily targets (170 lb, slight lean): ~3,000 kcal · 160–170g protein · 360–400g carbs · 80–90g fat. Eat full maintenance on long-run/quality days; the slight deficit lives on easy/rest days only.

Recovery & Bedtime Stretch

~10 min, do it before bed (gentle, parasympathetic — not a workout). Hold each stretch 45–60s, breathe slow. Band work is light activation, not strength.

Mobilize the calves & ankles

Protects your shin/achilles history
  • Standing gastroc calf stretch — 45s/side (back leg straight)
  • Bent-knee soleus stretch — 45s/side
  • Banded ankle dorsiflexion mobilization — 15 reps/side (knee-over-toe drive)

Open the hips & quads

Couch stretch is the highest-value runner stretch
  • Couch stretch (rear foot elevated, hip flexor + quad) — 60s/side
  • Figure-4 glute / piriformis stretch (lying) — 45s/side
  • Lying spinal twist (low back + IT band) — 45s/side

Hamstrings & light hip stability

Banded glute work helps the knee tweaks
  • Lying hamstring stretch with band (leg up, gentle) — 45s/side
  • Banded clamshells — 15 reps/side (slow, glute-med focus)
  • Glute bridge — 12 slow reps (squeeze 2s at top)

Wind down

  • Child’s pose with side reach — 60s
  • 4 slow nasal breaths, 4s in / 6s out — downshifts to sleep

After long runs, add 3–5 min of easy foam rolling on calves, quads, and glutes before this routine. Skip rolling on rest days — bedtime stretch alone is enough.

Race Day

  • Mi 1–6: 8:10–8:15. Feel held back. Bank patience.
  • Mi 7–20: Settle to 8:00. Gel every 20–25 min, water each station.
  • Mi 21–26.2: Hold 8:00 or push if legs are there.
  • SF hills: Effort over pace uphill; recover on the downs.
